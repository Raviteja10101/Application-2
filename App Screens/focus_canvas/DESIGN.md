## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the serene discipline of an archival reading room bathed in dawn light. Built for serious intellectual inquiry, research curation, and prolonged scholarly focus, it replaces high-friction digital artifice with the quiet tactility of heavy rag paper, hand-pressed folios, and antique bookbinding materials.

The style is an exacting synthesis of **Minimalism** and **Tactile Archival Craft**. It avoids skeuomorphic excess, translating the material sensibility of fine stationery, vellum manuscripts, and patinated brass instruments into precise, modern digital interfaces. The emotional tone is measured, authoritative, calm, and contemplative—deliberate without feeling archaic, prestigious without feeling exclusionary.

## Colors

The palette establishes an authentic daylight parchment foundation with deep pigment ink and burnished brass highlights:

- **Primary (`#2D2A26` / Espresso Charcoal):** Grounding ink tone used for primary commands, critical markers, active text, and structural anchors. Replaces harsh synthetic blacks with the depth of aged gall ink.
- **Secondary (`#B8860B` / Antique Burnished Brass):** The intellectual signature accent. Applied to focus indicators, active selection tabs, delicate rule accents, and verified citations.
- **Tertiary (`#6B665E` / Sepia Slate):** A muted, scholarly midtone for supporting metadata, marginalia, timestamps, and resting state icons.
- **Neutral (`#F4F2EB` / Warm Alabaster Linen):** The universal structural ground, echoing heavy rag paper and pale vellum.

### Supporting Color Roles
- **Canvas Base:** `#F9F8F5` (Morning Linen) transitioning down to `#ECE8DF` (Archival Vellum) for sunken or recessed panels.
- **Surface & Containers:** `#FFFFFF` to `#FAF9F6` (Pure Pressed Ivory) providing clean contrast against the parchment foundation.
- **Borders & Dividers:** Hairline strokes in `#E5E0D8` (Warm Limestone) and `#D8D2C5` (Aged Folio Edge).
- **Ink Tones:** Deepest headline ink `#1C1A17` for display tiers; supporting caption ink `#8C867D`.
- **System States:** Information callouts lean on deep indigo `#2C3E50` diluted on ivory; warnings utilize warm amber-umber `#A85D1A`; successes use botanical laurel `#3E5C43`.

## Typography

The typographic system pairs **Newsreader** for headings, structural chapter titling, and editorial cues, with **Literata** for long-form reading comfort, metadata, and component interfaces.

- **Editorial Hierarchy:** Newsreader carries literary authority, utilizing gentle optical weights and delicate serif curves that echo classic press work.
- **Reading Ergonomics:** Literata provides open counters, robust serifs, and exceptional readability at smaller digital scales, making dense reading effortless without optical fatigue.
- **Marginalia & Small Caps:** `label-sm` is suited for small-caps treatment with expanded tracking (`0.08em`) for section indices, library tags, and folio pagination.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is informed by classic book proportion canons and editorial broadsheet structure:

- **Grid System:** A responsive 12-column layout on desktop (`1280px` max content containment for dense layouts, `768px` single-column reading well for sustained essays and archival study). Mobile uses a single or dual-column layout with generous outer breathing room.
- **Margins & Gutters:** Desktop canvas margins are deliberate and expansive (`margin: 3rem`), mirroring wide paper margins where scholarly notes would naturally rest. Mobile contracts to `1.25rem`.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows a strict base-4 rhythm, scaled into generous component separation (`space-xl` and `space-2xl`) to prevent interface clutter and foster tranquil contemplation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through delicate surface stratification and warm, diffused ambient shadows rather than stark digital drop shadows.

- **Tonal Stratification:** The canvas rests on `#F9F8F5`. Working modules and cards occupy `#FAF9F6` or crisp `#FFFFFF`. Recessed zones, inset metadata drawers, and search tracks sit in sunken `#ECE8DF`.
- **Stone Outlines:** Surfaces are bounded by ultra-fine 1px borders in `#E5E0D8` (or `#D8D2C5` when floating). High-contrast digital borders are strictly avoided.
- **Organic Ambient Shadows:** 
  - *Resting Leaf (Cards & Containers):* `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(45, 42, 38, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(45, 42, 38, 0.02)`
  - *Elevated Folio (Dropdowns & Modals):* `0 12px 28px -6px rgba(45, 42, 38, 0.07), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(45, 42, 38, 0.03)`
- **Atmospheric Warmth:** Shadows are tinted with the primary espresso hue (`rgba(45, 42, 38, ...)`) rather than cold grey, preserving the warmth of paper illuminated by natural ambient light.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Soft** shape language (`roundedness: 1`). 

Corners reference freshly trimmed archival paper folios, stationery cards, and leather bound book corners rather than hyper-rounded or pill-shaped tech widgets:
- Standard UI elements (buttons, inputs, chips) carry a subtle `0.25rem` (4px) radius.
- Cards, panels, and archival folios use `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px).
- Dialogs and reference flyouts cap at `rounded-xl` (`0.75rem` / 12px).
- Pill shapes (`9999px`) are reserved exclusively for contextual status dots or bookmark pins.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep espresso charcoal (`#2D2A26`) background, warm linen text (`#FAF9F6`), 4px border radius. Hover introduces a subtle warm glow with deep brass edge tinting (`#8F6910`). Focus ring uses a 2px offset ring in `#B8860B`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background `#FAF9F6`, hairline border in `#D8D2C5`, text in `#2D2A26`. Hover shifts background to pure white `#FFFFFF` with border darkened to `#B8860B`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** No border or background. Typography in `#6B665E`, transitioning to `#1C1A17` with an underlying 1px brass flourish rule on hover.

### Inputs & Search Bars
- Background sits in `#FFFFFF` with a 1px border of `#E5E0D8`. 
- Placeholder text in `#8C867D` set in Literata italic. 
- Focus state shifts border color immediately to burnished brass `#B8860B` with an ambient glow: `0 0 0 3px rgba(184, 134, 11, 0.12)`.

### Cards & Scholarly Containers
- Background of warm ivory (`#FAF9F6`), surrounded by a 1px `#E5E0D8` perimeter stroke.
- Internal layout features generous padding (`space-lg`), separating bibliographic headings from commentary body copy with an optional 1px hairline divider tinted in `#F4F2EB`.

### Chips & Badges
- Compact geometry with 4px roundedness.
- Neutral state: tinted `#ECE8DF` ground with `#6B665E` label typography.
- Active/Selected state: pale brass wash background (`rgba(184, 134, 11, 0.10)`), `#8F6910` border, and deep `#2D2A26` text.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Base control: 1px border in `#D8D2C5` over an ivory ground.
- Checked state: solid deep espresso (`#2D2A26`) fill with an antique brass checkmark glyph or center pip (`#B8860B`).

### Lists & Citations
- Academic list entries feature light baseline dividers (`#E5E0D8`).
- Hover state over interactive citations produces a delicate pale wash (`#F4F2EB`) with a left margin accent pip in `#B8860B`.
